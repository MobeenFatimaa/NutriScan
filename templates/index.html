How NutriScan Operates

From image capture to custom neural classification, discover how our vision intelligence processes your meal in seconds.

01

Capture & Upload

Upload any photo of your dish. Our client workspace handles instant preview rendering, validation, and secure payload transfer to the Flask backend.

02

MobileNetV3 Classification

Our fine-tuned MobileNetV3-Large PyTorch vision model normalizes tensors and classifies regional dishes with high confidence.

03

Nutritional Dashboard

Instantly view calories, macronutrient breakdowns, micronutrient profiles, and session history logged automatically in real time.

Platform Capabilities

Built for Automated Precision

An end-to-end Computer Vision and Flask pipeline designed for high-accuracy food classification and detailed macro breakdown.

PyTorch

MobileNetV3 Classification

Leverages a lightweight PyTorch vision network trained to accurately recognize 80+ regional dishes.

Macros

Macro & Micro Extraction

Automates complete breakdown of protein, carbs, fats, dietary fiber, and key micronutrient profiles.

Session

Session Meal History

Automatically retains your 5 most recent analyzed meals directly in Flask session memory.

Softmax

Confidence Scoring

Executes Softmax scoring to evaluate model prediction certainty before generating reports.

Secure

Client Payload Safety

Secures uploads via UUID filename hashing, strict extension checks, and 16MB file caps.

Tailwind

Glassmorphism UI

Custom-styled with Tailwind CSS, backdrop filters, responsive grids, and instant previews.
